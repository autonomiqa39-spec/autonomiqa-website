How It Works

The 8-stage engine — what runs automatically

One connected system that handles every step from target identification to personalised outreach.

01

ICP Configuration

Encodes your ideal customer profile — industry, role, size, geography — into structured targeting logic.

Fixes: vague, assumption-based targeting
02

Automated Prospect Discovery

Continuously identifies ICP-aligned prospects. No manual list building. No stale data. Always rolling.

Fixes: discovery stopping when people stop
03

Data Enrichment

Augments every prospect record with firmographic and verified contact data before outreach begins.

Fixes: shallow data, fragmented tools
04

Signal Intelligence

Monitors funding events, hiring spikes, leadership changes, and intent signals in real time.

Fixes: right company, wrong moment
05

Lead Scoring

Applies standardised logic to rank prospects by fit and readiness. High-value leads prioritised automatically.

Fixes: all leads treated equally
06

Hyper-Personalisation

Uses enriched and signal data to tailor every message at scale. Personal without being slow.

Fixes: generic, copy-paste outreach
07

Automated Outreach

Triggers outbound and follow-up sequences automatically. Consistent cadence, zero manual effort.

Fixes: forgotten follow-ups, volume ceiling
08

Measurement & Refinement

Full funnel visibility — what’s working, where prospects drop, which ICPs convert best.

Fixes: no attribution, no feedback loop
